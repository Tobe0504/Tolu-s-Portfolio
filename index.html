03 / HOW I WORK

Four moves,in this order.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Interviews, support tickets, session replays. I start where the friction actually lives, not where the roadmap says it does.

  2. 02

    Frame

    A problem stated well is half solved. I write the one-sentence version and get everyone to argue with it.

  3. 03

    Build

    Real prototypes in real code. Opinions get cheap the moment you can click the thing.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Then watch it. The design isn’t finished until the numbers and the humans both agree.
